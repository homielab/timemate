TimeMate 3.7 lets the break screen wait during calls and makes the timer and statistics more reliable.

New
• Wait during calls and video: turn it on in Settings → Alerts & Sound, and the break screen waits while a Zoom, Meet or Teams call, screen sharing or a video keeps the display awake. The notification still arrives.
• Undo Last Session in Settings → Statistics removes only the focus session recorded last, so one pomodoro counted by mistake no longer means resetting all statistics.

Fixes
• A timer left idle overnight, or a Mac that slept through a focus session, now shows a fresh focus session, so Start runs what you see.
• A session paused in the evening and finished the next morning counts for the day it ran.
• A label typed after pressing Start is saved with that focus session.
• The "daily goal reached" notification shows again after you raise the goal, undo a session or reset statistics.
• Shortcuts Add Time returns the minutes it added, and the notification at the end of a break no longer skips your focus session.
• Better Vietnamese translation and VoiceOver labels.
