TimeMate 3.10 fixes the menu bar icon, restarts, the break screen and the By hour statistics.

Fixes
• Opening TimeMate again brings back its menu bar icon if you dragged it out. It can't hide forever.
• Restarting to change the language or after Reset All Settings no longer records the session twice.
• The break screen comes back when displays reconnect, and a break picked up after a restart keeps its skip delay.
• Statistics By hour spreads a paused session over the hours it really ran.
