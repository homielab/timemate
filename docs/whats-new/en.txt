TimeMate 3.8 gives Shortcuts richer timer data and makes breaks, statistics and notifications more reliable.

New
• The Shortcuts actions Get Timer Status and Get Today's Progress now return details you can branch on: the timer's state, session, minutes and seconds left and label; today's pomodoros, focus minutes, goal and streak.
• Statistics follow your Mac's region and 12/24-hour clock in the app's language.

Fixes
• Quitting mid-session (or updating, logging out, shutting down) pauses the session instead of counting it as finished, so your history and streak only hold time you worked.
• Skip on a session that hasn't started only moves to the next session, and Restart asks before throwing away focus sessions already done.
• The Skip button delay now holds for every way out of a break, including Reset, the menu bar menu, notifications and Shortcuts.
• When the break screen waits for a call or a full-screen app, the alarm sound waits too.
• After the break screen or popover closes, the app you were using gets the keyboard back.
• Settings fit small screens, the break screen stays steady when displays change, and ⌘ alone with a letter can no longer be taken as a shortcut.
• The "daily goal reached" notification now also comes for a session that ended just before midnight.
• CSV export keeps labels as typed, quoting only those a spreadsheet would run as formulas.
• Better VoiceOver labels and many smaller fixes.
