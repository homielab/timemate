TimeMate 3.6 is a big update: more control over your sessions, richer statistics, Shortcuts and Siri, and many fixes.

Timer
• Set any session length from 1 to 180 minutes, for example 50/10 or a 90-minute focus.
• +5 min adds five minutes to a running or paused focus session when you are in the flow, or makes a break longer.
• Name what you are working on with "What are you working on?", and pick it again from your recent labels.
• Separate switches for auto-start of breaks and of focus sessions.
• Optional: pause the focus session when the Mac sleeps or the screen locks.
• Restart asks for confirmation while a session is in progress.

Statistics
• Set a daily goal and follow your streak of days that reached it.
• See the last 7 or 30 days, your daily average, your best day and your focus time per label.
• Export a daily CSV for 7 days, 30 days or the full 90-day history, or every session with its label.

Menu bar and keyboard
• Global shortcuts from any app: Start/Pause, Skip, Reset, and Add 5 Minutes (⌃⌥E). Turn them on or record your own in Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.
• Space starts or pauses in the popover.
• Right-click the menu bar item for a quick menu, and hover it to see the time left.
• Choose how the menu bar shows the time: full time, minutes only, or icon only.

Shortcuts and Siri
• New actions: Start Timer, Start Focus, Start Focus with Label, Pause Timer, Skip Session, Add Time, Get Timer Status and Get Today's Progress.

Break screen and alerts
• Choose how long the Skip button waits, and postpone or extend a break.
• The break screen waits while you watch a full-screen video or present.
• A silent alarm option and a separate sound for breaks.
• Notifications tell you what comes next, and still arrive after the Mac sleeps or TimeMate quits.

Accessibility
• Better VoiceOver labels across the popover, Settings and the break screen, and support for Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast.

Fixes and performance
• Less CPU and battery use, especially during breaks and when the screen is off.
• The running session survives quitting, updates and restarts.
• Start at Login, Keep Mac awake and Keep window on top now work reliably.
• Many fixes to the long-break cycle, statistics, CSV export and the Vietnamese translation.
